## Starting a WeatherBug Radar Session

Begin by selecting the location you want to monitor and reviewing the available forecast and radar information. Check timestamps and local alerts before using weather data to make time-sensitive decisions.

1. **Open the Weather Service:** Access the verified WeatherBug application or supported web interface.
2. **Select a Location:** Choose the city or area relevant to your plans.
3. **Review Current Conditions:** Check the displayed temperature and other available weather measurements.
4. **Explore Radar:** Open the radar view and review its map scale and timestamp.
5. **Check Forecast Details:** Compare hourly conditions with the longer-range outlook.
6. **Review Alerts:** Check relevant official warnings before outdoor activities or travel.

**Weather safety note:** Forecasts and radar imagery can be incomplete or change quickly. Follow official weather warnings and local emergency instructions when hazardous conditions are possible.
